MEDIUM 1 - A UTM coordinate far from its central meridian is wrong by
centimetres while the record says "The coordinate is computed correctly".

  Where: michspc/spc/convert.py:750-770 (_check_extent's message, now reached
  by UTM zones), michspc/spc/zones.py:1141-1145 (UTM extent = the 6-degree
  band), engine michspc/spc/tm.py (the manual's section 3.23/3.24 series).

  The manual's TM series is accurate inside a UTM band but degrades fast
  outside it, and the INVERSE much faster than the forward. Measured against
  PROJ 9.8.1 / EPSG 6344-6346 (whose Poder/Engsager series is accurate to
  nanometres at these distances), at Michigan positions, worst over 41.7-47.5 N:

    17N used at  -86.0: fwd 0.1 mm  inv  0.4 mm
    17N used at  -88.0: fwd 0.7 mm  inv  5.5 mm
    17N used at  -89.0: fwd 2.0 mm  inv 16.2 mm
    17N used at  -90.4: fwd 6.8 mm  inv 60.6 mm
    15N used at  -86.0: fwd 0.7 mm  inv  5.5 mm
    15N used at  -84.0: fwd 4.9 mm  inv 42.4 mm
    15N used at -82.42: fwd 17.3 mm inv 159.8 mm
    (16N anywhere in Michigan, <= 4.6 deg: <= 0.2 mm - fine.)

  Concrete failing input (through job.run and the written ZIP):
    GEODETIC_TO_ZONE, NAD83(2011), metres, target 15N, row
    "2,42.0,-82.5,200,Q" writes N 4703441.2095 E 1370080.9937, grid scale
    1.00892914. PROJ: N 4703441.1954 E 1370080.9866, scale 1.00892868.
    Wrong by 14.1 mm / 7.1 mm and 0.46 ppm (printed digits 6-8 of the factor
    are wrong). Inverse of PROJ's coordinate through MCX lands 0.139 m south
    of the true position. A ZONE_TO_ZONE job 17N -> 2113 (ift) over a lattice
    spanning -90.3..-82.6 differed from PROJ by 0.417 ft in northing.
    The job record's WARNINGS block for that point says: "lies outside the
    area NAD83(2011) / UTM zone 15N covers (41.6 to 48.4 N, -96.0 to -90.0 E).
    The coordinate is computed correctly, but distortion grows with distance
    from the zone" - a false sentence in a sealed record for a UTM job.
    (The plan's section 2 says "far outside the band the existing extent
    warning fires"; it fires, but it asserts correctness.)

  The configuration is unusual (a UTM zone used 5-10 deg from its meridian)
  but it is accepted, written and certified. No test exercises a UTM zone
  beyond 4 deg from its meridian (test_every_zone_agrees_with_proj_over_michigan
  stops at meridian +/- 4), so nothing would notice.

  Suggested fix (any one): refuse a UTM point beyond a measured distance from
  the meridian (the inverse passes 0.5 mm at about 5.1 deg; 4.6 deg covers all
  of Michigan in 16N) naming the measured error; or give UTM zones a warning
  that does not claim correctness and states the measured error; or replace
  the UTM path with the Krueger n-series (Karney 2011, 6th order), holding it
  to the same 246 NCAT anchors in band and to PROJ out of band. Pin with the
  42.0/-82.5 15N input above and a far-band inverse.
