Summary of Steps for Positioning in the 
Modernized NSRS
1. Process GNSS baselines at the time of the measurements (e.g., 
2026.25) using latest ITRF2020 products
2. Reduce GNSS data into dX, dY, dZ vector components
3. Transform results to ITRF2020 at epoch 2020.00 by applying 
IFDM2022 (deformation grid)
4. Constrain published control (e.g., CORS) coordinates in ITRF2020 
epoch 2020.00
5. Perform least squares adjustment. Report adjusted coordinates
Note: since Δt = 0, coordinates in ITRF2020 = NATRF2022 at 
epoch 2020.00!
