Michigan

Which of Michigan’s 18 SPCS2022 low-distortion zones covers each county. Every county carries its name and its zone code.

Michigan also has one statewide zone, 260001 MI, oblique Mercator, and it covers all 83 counties. It is stated here rather than drawn, because as a nineteenth fill it would cover every other zone on the map.
