Linear Distortion Maps

Below are maps showing linear distortion and performance statistics for SPCS2022 zones (click on the maps to get higher resolution versions). Maps of existing SPCS 83 zones are also provided for comparison with SPCS2022.

Not all maps use the same increment for distortion symbology, because distortion can vary greatly in different zones. However, the symbology is the same for adjacent SPCS2022 and SPCS 83 maps to facilitate comparison.
