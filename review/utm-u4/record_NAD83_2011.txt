HOW THIS SOFTWARE IS VERIFIED

The UTM zone constants are not taken on trust. UTM is defined by constants
every zone shares - scale 0.9996 on the central meridian, a false easting of
500,000 m, a false northing of 0 m at the equator - and by its central
meridian, 6z - 183 degrees for zone z. This software stores those and derives
the rest; its test suite holds each stored zone to them.

The conversion itself is checked against the National Geodetic Survey's own
Coordinate Conversion and Transformation Tool (NCAT). Two hundred twelve
positions across Michigan in UTM zones 16 and 17 were converted by production
NCAT in NAD83(2011) (captured 2026-10-22), and those results are held in the
test suite as fixed reference values. This software reproduces their northing,
easting, convergence and scale factor within the precision NCAT prints,
converting both to and from the grid.

Zone 15, which covers only the western tip of Gogebic County, has no NCAT
position in Michigan. It is the same projection with its central meridian six
degrees west of zone 16's, and the suite holds it to zone 16 at points the
same distance from their meridians.

The NCAT checks compare this software against NGS, not against itself.
